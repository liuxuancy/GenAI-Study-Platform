Every course on the platform — preset with the deployment, or generated by an instructor through the AI toolbox and published themselves. The only lever here is whether students can see the course.

Course Instructor Created Source Status Actions

Newest first. A removal is reversible at any time.

Removing a course takes it out of the student catalogue and nowhere else. It stays in its instructor’s own list, its chapters and exercises are untouched, and students who were part-way through keep their progress for the day it comes back. Course content is not editable here — this screen can only change visibility, which is why there is no edit button in any row.
